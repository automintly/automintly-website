Cookie Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

Current status: optional analytics are disabled. Automintly does not currently load advertising pixels or cross-site behavioral tracking on this site.

What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small pieces of data stored by a browser. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, and scripts that remember a choice or measure use of a site.

Technology currently used

CategoryPurpose and duration
Hosting and securityCloudflare, Render, or Netlify may use request data or necessary security technology to deliver the site, prevent abuse, and process the inquiry form. Duration is controlled by those providers and the security need.
Consent choiceIf optional analytics are enabled, automintly_cookie_choice_v1 records “analytics” or “necessary” for up to 180 days so the site can remember your choice.
Optional analyticsDisabled as of the effective date. If enabled later, the configured provider will not load until you select “Allow optional analytics.”

Do we need a consent banner now?

Because optional analytics and advertising pixels are currently disabled, the site does not show a banner merely to announce necessary delivery and security technology. The code is designed to display a choice before optional analytics load if tracking is enabled later. Legal requirements vary by visitor location and by the technology used, so this conclusion must be reviewed whenever a tracker, chat widget, video embed, advertising pixel, or new provider is added.

Your controls

You can remove or block cookies in browser settings. If optional analytics are enabled later, the consent banner will offer “Allow optional analytics” and “Use necessary cookies only.” An enabled Global Privacy Control signal or Do Not Track value of “1” prevents optional analytics from loading.

External links

Links to appointment scheduling and other third-party sites are not embedded trackers on Automintly’s pages. Those services may use their own cookies after you follow the link, subject to their policies.

Changes and contact

We update this policy when our technology or providers change. Questions may be sent to automintly@gmail.com.
