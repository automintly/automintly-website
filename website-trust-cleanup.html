Common questions

Small project. Clear boundaries.

Do you need my password?

No. Never send a password by email. When possible, invite Automintly as a limited collaborator or user. The written scope will explain the safest available access method for your platform.

What if my site needs more than five fixes?

We will separate the first five eligible fixes from any optional additional work. Extra work is quoted separately and never added automatically.

Does this include a redesign?

No. This offer preserves the current design and corrects the agreed visible issues. A redesign is a separate project.

When does the 48-hour window begin?

After the written scope is accepted and the required website access is working. Delays caused by missing access or third-party approval do not count toward the work window.
