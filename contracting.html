Eligibility check

We should be able to explain why your business fits.

If the evidence does not support a realistic contracting path, we say so instead of filling your dashboard with irrelevant notices.

A stronger starting fit

  • You already deliver a clearly defined product or service.
  • You can document capacity, service area, credentials, and past work.
  • You can meet required insurance, licensing, security, or delivery conditions.
  • You will maintain the registrations required for opportunities you choose to pursue.
  • An authorized person can review and approve every submission.

Not ready yet

  • The business is still an idea without an operating capability.
  • The required registrations, licenses, or delivery capacity cannot be supported.
  • You want automated bids without reviewing the solicitation or final response.
  • You expect Automintly to guarantee an award or influence an agency decision.
  • The work would require claims that cannot be verified.
Important fee and submission boundary

No upfront search fee does not mean hidden promises.

Automintly clients pay no Contracting add-on fee before a qualifying award. Any award-based success fee is considered only where legally permitted and agreed in writing before bid support begins. No standard percentage is currently quoted or approved.

Federal rules restrict contingent-fee arrangements, so Automintly does not promise that it can obtain an award, use improper influence, or submit a response without the customer’s final authorization.

Dashboard access remains a separate scope-based monthly fee. Registration, legal, certification, insurance, and other third-party costs remain the customer’s responsibility.

Official resources SAM.gov entity registration SAM.gov states that businesses applying directly for federal awards need an active registration and receive a Unique Entity ID through that process. FAR Subpart 3.4 — Contingent Fees The federal acquisition rules define and restrict contingent-fee arrangements for obtaining government contracts.
Common questions

Know the boundaries before you start.

Do I need a SAM.gov registration?

You can explore whether your capabilities fit before registration is complete. To apply directly for federal awards as a prime awardee, SAM.gov says your entity needs an active registration. Registration also assigns the Unique Entity ID used in the process.

Does Automintly submit bids automatically?

No. The service organizes reviewed opportunities, requirements, evidence, and draft bid-support material. Your authorized team reviews the solicitation, approves the response, and controls submission.

Do you guarantee that we will win a contract?

No. Awards depend on the solicitation, competition, eligibility, agency evaluation, pricing, past performance, and many factors Automintly does not control.

What do I pay upfront?

There is no Contracting search or setup charge for Automintly clients. Dashboard access is separate. A success fee would apply only if legally permitted, documented in a signed agreement before bid support, and triggered by the qualifying award defined in that agreement.
