How the path works

No lead should depend on memory alone.

  1. An existing inbound inquiry enters the approved path.
  2. Eligibility and permission checks run before any message.
  3. The lead is assigned to a real employee, with a backup route.
  4. Failures, opt-outs, and unanswered cases remain visible.

Before we start

Questions worth answering up front.

When could setup be delivered?

The target is five business days after complete intake, access, approvals, and any required sender registration. We confirm a realistic schedule before work begins.

Do you send messages to every lead?

No. Only eligible inbound leads go through approved paths. Unknown permissions fail closed, opt-outs stop sends, and your staff handles exceptions.

Who handles customer conversations?

Your named employee and backup. The system supports acknowledgement, limited questions, and handoff; it does not replace a person for complex or urgent situations.

How does billing work?

The listed scope is $995 setup plus $349 per month for ongoing monitoring and bounded maintenance. Provider charges are separate. We confirm fit and issue the appropriate invoices before work starts.
