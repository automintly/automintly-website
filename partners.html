Partner process

Know what happens before access changes hands.

Step 1

Fit check

Describe one client process and the desired business outcome. No account access is needed yet.

Step 2

Written scope

We document the build, exclusions, responsibilities, acceptance test, timing, and wholesale price.

Step 3

Build and review

After payment and authorized access, we build in stages and stop at agreed review gates.

Step 4

Handoff

You receive test evidence, operating notes, known limits, and the agreed support boundary.

Clear boundaries

A practical fit for defined client work.

Good fit

  • A web, brand, marketing, or operations partner with an active client need
  • One process with a clear owner and an observable acceptance test
  • Supported tools, authorized access, and a realistic review schedule
  • A partner who wants documented delivery rather than a black box

Not a fit

  • Guaranteed revenue, rankings, or customer response
  • Credential sharing, platform rule evasion, or undisclosed account access
  • Unbounded builds without written scope and acceptance criteria
  • Production changes without the responsible account owner's approval
