Accessibility

Last reviewed: September 14, 2026

Automintly aims to make automintly.com usable by people with disabilities and to align its public pages with WCAG 2.2 Level AA where reasonably practicable. This is an ongoing effort, not a claim of formal certification.

Measures we take

  • Semantic headings, landmarks, labels, and native controls.
  • Keyboard-operable forms and visible focus indicators.
  • Text and controls designed for readable color contrast.
  • Reduced-motion support for visitors who request it.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images and hidden treatment for decorative media.
  • Clear link and button names that describe their purpose.
  • Automated checks plus manual keyboard and responsive-page review before release.

Known limitations

Third-party sites reached through external links, including appointment scheduling, are controlled by their providers. If you encounter a barrier on an Automintly page or cannot use an external option, contact us and we will provide a reasonable alternative where possible.

Accessibility feedback

Email automintly@gmail.com with “Accessibility” in the subject. Please include the page address, what you were trying to do, the barrier you encountered, and your preferred contact method. Do not include sensitive personal or health information.
