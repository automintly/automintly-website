## Rules before adding or publishing media

- Record the creator, source URL or generation service, creation/export date, applicable license, and proof of payment if relevant.
- Confirm the license permits commercial website, advertising, editing, and redistribution in the intended territory.
- Obtain written model and property releases when recognizable people, private property, trademarks, or customer locations require them.
- Obtain written permission for every client-provided logo, photo, testimonial, review, audio clip, or video.
- Do not treat search-engine results, social posts, or “found online” media as licensed.
- Keep material that merely inspires a design separate from production assets; do not reproduce protected characters, logos, or distinctive artwork.
- Remove an asset or replace it with verified original/licensed media if its provenance cannot be established.
