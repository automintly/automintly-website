60-second self-check

Can customers trust what they see?

Review eight visible details that can make a website harder to understand, trust, or use. This is a practical screen, not a promise of rankings, bookings, or revenue.

Runs only in your browser. Nothing is submitted.
0 of 8 checked
1. Are the phone number, email, and address consistent everywhere?

Compare the header, footer, contact page, booking page, and Google Business Profile.

2. Do business hours agree across every public page?

Watch for old seasonal hours, conflicting closed days, and outdated booking availability.

3. Is there one obvious next step on the main pages?

A visitor should quickly understand whether to book, call, request a quote, or buy.

4. Do the important links and buttons reach the right destination?

Test navigation, booking, contact, social, map, policy, and purchase links.

5. Does the site fit and remain readable on a phone?

Check for clipped text, sideways scrolling, tiny buttons, and content hidden behind menus.

6. Is the site clear of demo text, duplicate sections, and placeholder content?

Look for sample addresses, generic FAQs, repeated forms, and unfinished copy.

7. Does every public page load securely over HTTPS?

The browser should not display a connection warning or load an insecure version.

8. Are forms and controls clearly labeled?

Visitors should understand what each field requests and what each button will do.
